The complete, itemised list of every third-party open-source component shipped
in this application - component name, version, and license - is in
OSS-NOTICES.txt (machine-readable form: OSS-NOTICES.json). It is generated from

guest Linux kernel, Alpine guest packages), and every bundled native shared
